  <section style="margin-bottom: 2rem;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 1rem; color: var(--text-primary);">1. File and text processing</h2>
    <p>DataFrog's tools are designed to process supported inputs in your browser. The site does not provide a DataFrog server-side file-processing endpoint. When a tool reads a local file through the browser's file APIs, the file is read by that page in your browser. Tool-specific behaviour, supported formats, and limitations can differ, so review the instructions on the tool page before relying on an output.</p>
    <p>This description of file processing is not a promise that the website makes no network requests. A page may load scripts, styles, fonts, analytics, or advertising resources from third parties. If you handle confidential, regulated, or otherwise sensitive material, consider whether using a website that loads third-party resources is appropriate for your requirements.</p>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 2rem;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 1rem; color: var(--text-primary);">2. Analytics and third-party resources</h2>
    <p>At the time this policy was checked, the shared page template loads Ahrefs Analytics and the site layout includes an advertising script hosted by <code>profitableratecpmnetwork.com</code>. These resources cause the browser to contact third-party servers. Depending on the provider and its configuration, a request may expose technical information such as an IP address, browser and device details, referring page, and information about the request; a provider may also use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies.</p>
    <p>DataFrog does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. Review the providers' current privacy and cookie information for details about their collection, retention, and opt-out options. The presence of a third-party script should not be interpreted as evidence that DataFrog's tool input is sent to that provider; input handling must be assessed separately for each tool.</p>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 2rem;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 1rem; color: var(--text-primary);">3. Accounts and information sent to us</h2>
    <p>DataFrog does not currently require an account to use its public tools. If you contact us, the information you choose to include in your message will be received so we can respond. Do not include passwords, confidential files, or other sensitive information in a support request unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.</p>
    <p>We do not operate a server-side upload service for processing the files selected in the tools. This statement does not cover information that a browser sends to third-party resources or information you voluntarily send through contact channels.</p>
  </section>

  <section style="margin-bottom: 2rem;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 1rem; color: var(--text-primary);">4. Cookies and advertising choices</h2>
    <p>Third-party analytics or advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies, depending on their implementation and your browser settings. You can review and manage cookies in your browser. Blocking third-party resources may affect some website features or advertising, but it does not change how a tool's local file-reading code is designed to work.</p>

This page describes the implementation visible in the repository at the date shown; it is not a substitute for legal advice or a complete audit of every browser request.</p>
