What is a Bank Statement Analyzer?

A bank statement analyzer is a financial processing tool that examines bank account exports, transaction logs, and financial statements to automatically extract structured metrics, categorize spending, track income streams, and calculate cash flow health.

Instead of manually scrolling through hundreds of transaction lines or building custom formulas in spreadsheets, an automated analyzer groups transactions into recognizable spending buckets — such as groceries, rent, utilities, insurance, and subscriptions. It highlights recurring payments, surfaces potential duplicate charges, and computes overall financial health scores.

By performing all processing completely in the browser, you gain total clarity over your financial data without sharing private account details or credentials with third-party servers.

Why Analyze Bank Statements?

Identify Hidden Subscriptions

Spot forgotten recurring charges, streaming services, and automatic membership renewals that quietly drain monthly cash flow.

Monitor Net Cash Flow

Track deposit velocity against total withdrawals to maintain positive net cash flow and prevent unintended overdrafts.

Audit Expense Categories

Categorize spending into essential vs non-essential categories to uncover major cost reduction opportunities.

Detect Duplicate Transactions

Flag double-processed payments or repeated charges caused by merchant processing retries or system errors.

Tax & Expense Preparation

Export organized, categorized transaction summaries ready for annual tax filing or business expense reimbursements.

Assess Financial Health

Evaluate savings ratios, income stability, and expense distribution to set realistic budgeting benchmarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a bank statement analyzer and how does it work?

A bank statement analyzer processes raw transaction files to categorize spending, track income, detect recurring subscriptions, and evaluate cash flow stability.

Is my financial data uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens 100% inside your local web browser session. No data is uploaded to servers or stored in any cloud database.

Which file formats can I upload for analysis?

OFX, QFX, QIF, CSV, TSV, Excel (.xlsx/.xls), OpenDocument (.ods), JSON, XML, and pasted text tables.

How does automatic transaction categorization work?

The analyzer matches merchant descriptions to 20+ spending categories (Groceries, Utilities, Rent, Salary, Restaurants, etc.). You can also manually edit categories in the transaction table.

How are recurring payments detected?

The engine scans payees and transaction amounts across time to flag monthly charges such as streaming subscriptions, utility bills, rent payments, and regular salary deposits.
