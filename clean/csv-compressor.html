How to Compress a CSV File Online

A CSV compressor helps you reduce CSV file size by removing unnecessary empty rows and empty columns. It's perfect for cleaning up exported data, logs, or large datasets before sharing or uploading.

This tool works 100% in your browser – your file is never uploaded to any server. It reads the CSV, strips out all blank lines and useless columns, and gives you the cleaned version. You can then download the result as a plain .csv, or as a compressed .csv.gz (GZIP) or .csv.bz2 (BZIP2) file for even smaller sizes.

Why Use a CSV Compressor?

  • Save storage space – compress large CSV files before archiving.
  • Faster uploads – smaller files upload quicker to cloud services.
  • Privacy first – all processing is client‑side; nothing leaves your computer.
  • Multiple formats – download as plain CSV, GZIP, or BZIP2.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it change the actual data?

No. The tool only removes rows where every cell is empty and columns where every cell is empty. Your real data stays intact.

Can I compress a very large CSV file?

Yes – the tool uses streaming‑like processing and can handle files up to several hundred MB, depending on your browser's memory. For extremely large files, consider splitting them first.

What’s the difference between GZIP and BZIP2?

GZIP is faster and more widely supported; BZIP2 often compresses slightly better but is slower. Both are standard compression formats for CSV files.
