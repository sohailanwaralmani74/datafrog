QIF to Excel Converter

Convert a Quicken Interchange Format (QIF) statement into an Excel workbook for reviewing, sorting, and filtering transactions. Processing stays in your browser.

🔒 Your files are processed in your browser. DataFrog does not upload your content.

Choose a file or paste your content, then select Convert to Excel.

How to convert QIF to Excel

  1. Choose your .qif statement file, or paste its text.
  2. Select Convert to Excel. The converter creates an .xlsx workbook with a Transactions sheet.
  3. Review the CSV preview and compare sample transactions with the original statement.

What is included?

The workbook contains transaction dates, amounts, payees, memos, categories, account type, transaction type, check numbers, cleared status, and IDs when available. Split-category breakdowns are not exported as separate rows. Check split transactions against the original QIF file before relying on category totals.

Before reconciling

QIF exports can differ between banks and personal finance programs. Check row counts, signs on debit and credit amounts, and dates before using the workbook for accounting or reconciliation.
