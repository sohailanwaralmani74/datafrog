How to convert CSV to YAML

  1. Paste your CSV text or choose a local CSV file.
  2. Choose the field delimiter used in your data and indicate whether the first row contains headers.
  3. Select Convert to YAML. Review the result, then copy it or download the converted file.

What happens to your data?

The conversion runs in your browser. CSV fields enclosed in double quotes are parsed, including commas inside quoted values and escaped double quotes. Check the output before using it in another application, especially when the CSV contains unusual line breaks, duplicate column names, or values that require a specific data type.

CSV conversion tips

Make sure the delimiter matches your source file. If the first row is actual data rather than column names, turn off the header option. Keep a copy of the original CSV so you can compare the converted output and confirm that all rows and columns were preserved.
