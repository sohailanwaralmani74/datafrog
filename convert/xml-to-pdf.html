Why Convert XML to PDF?

While XML excels at structured machine-to-machine exchange, it is completely unreadable for clients, executive boards, auditors, and patients. Converting XML into a formatted PDF document transforms cold tags into human-centric visual documents featuring formatted titles, clean metadata banners, and styled tabular sections.
