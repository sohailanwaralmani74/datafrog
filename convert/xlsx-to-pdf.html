Why Convert Excel to PDF?

Converting Excel spreadsheets to PDF is essential for sharing data in a fixed, print‑ready format. PDFs preserve formatting, ensure consistent display across devices, and are universally accepted for reports, invoices, and official documents. This tool transforms your .xlsx or .xls files into clean, paginated PDFs with customisable options – perfect for professional use.

How to Use the Excel to PDF Converter

  1. Upload – drag & drop your Excel file or click to browse.
  2. Adjust settings – choose orientation (portrait/landscape), page size, font size, and whether to include headers.
  3. Generate – click Generate PDF to create the document.
  4. Preview & Download – view the PDF in the embedded preview and download it with one click.

The PDF preserves numeric and text values, with automatic column sizing and pagination. Large datasets are split across multiple pages with repeating table headers for clarity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool support multiple sheets?

Only the first sheet is converted. You can select a different sheet from the dropdown after uploading.

Can I customise the page layout?

Yes – choose between portrait and landscape orientation, and select from A4, Letter, Legal, A3, or A5 page sizes.

How are large tables handled?

The tool automatically paginates large tables and repeats the header row on each page for easy reading.
