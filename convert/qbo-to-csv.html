QBO STATEMENT CONVERTER

QBO to CSV Converter

Convert a QuickBooks Online (QBO) bank statement into a CSV transaction table. Extract posted dates, amounts, payees, memos, and available transaction IDs locally in your browser.

🔒 Your files are processed in your browser. DataFrog does not upload your content.

Choose a file or paste your content, then select Convert to CSV.

How to convert QBO to CSV

  1. Choose a Quicken/QuickBooks Bank Online (.qbo) statement, or paste its contents.
  2. Select Convert to CSV to extract the statement transactions.
  3. Review the output and download the CSV for a spreadsheet or data import.

Transaction columns

The export includes posted date, amount, payee, memo, category when present, account type, transaction type, check number, and FITID transaction ID. The original statement may include additional bank metadata that is not part of the CSV transaction table.

Verify before importing

QBO files commonly use OFX-style statement records. Compare transaction totals, dates, and debit/credit signs against your source file. This converter does not connect to your bank or submit financial information anywhere.
