    <h2>About Sohail</h2>
    <p>Sohail Anwar is a Software Engineer with 12 years of experience in software development. His work at DataFrog includes building and evaluating browser-based document and data workflows, with particular attention to whether an implementation behaves correctly with real files rather than only with ideal examples.</p>
    <p>He contributes engineering-focused explanations to DataFrog articles, especially where a user needs to understand what a conversion or processing step can realistically preserve, change, or lose.</p>
  </section>

    <ul>

  <section>
    <h2>How he approaches technical content</h2>
    <p>Sohail treats a conversion tool as a complete workflow rather than a button that produces a file. The useful question is whether the output can actually be opened, edited, searched, shared, printed, or reused for its intended purpose.</p>
    <p>For document conversion, this means paying attention to things such as text order, page boundaries, images, tables, fonts, headings, and the difference between a text PDF and a scanned page. When a limitation is real, the content should say so rather than promising perfect fidelity.</p>
