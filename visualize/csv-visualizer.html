Frequently Asked Questions

How does the CSV Visualizer convert tabular data into charts?

The CSV Visualizer detects header columns in your CSV dataset, allowing you to select X-axis categories and Y-axis numerical metrics to render responsive Bar, Line, Area, or Pie charts.

Is my CSV dataset uploaded to external servers?

No, 100% private. All CSV parsing, column auto-detection, Chart.js canvas rendering, and PNG exports execute locally inside your web browser RAM.

What chart types are supported?

The CSV Visualizer supports Bar Charts, Line Charts, Area Charts, Pie Charts, Doughnut Charts, and Scatter Plots.
