What is a Flowchart Builder?

A Flowchart Builder is an interactive diagramming application that enables users to visually model business processes, software workflows, decision paths, and organizational structures directly inside a web browser.

By translating abstract procedures into standardized geometric shapes — such as start pills, process rectangles, decision diamonds, and database cylinders — flowcharts make complex operations instantly understandable for teams, stakeholders, and clients.

Everything in this online flowchart maker executes locally on your device. You can design, edit, and export vector diagrams without signing up or uploading data to third-party servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an online Flowchart Builder and how does it work?

An online Flowchart Builder is a visual canvas that lets you design, connect, and export flowcharts, process maps, and org charts directly inside your browser without installing software.

Is my diagram data stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. All rendering, editing, and file exports run 100% locally inside your web browser. No diagram content is transmitted to servers.

What export formats are supported?

You can export diagrams as vector SVG images, high-res PNG pictures, JSON files (for saving and re-opening), or printable HTML reports.

Can I save my diagram and open it again later?

Yes! Click Save JSON to download your diagram file. Later, click Load JSON to resume editing anytime.
